# Maruti Suzuki Sales Performance Analysis 2025

## Project Overview

This project analyses Maruti Suzuki sales data from January to December
2025. It is a Data Analyst portfolio project that uses Microsoft Excel
and Power BI to present sales performance through KPIs, charts, and
dashboards.

The goal is to understand which car models and regions contribute most
to sales, track monthly trends, and identify areas where business
performance and customer experience could improve.

## Objectives

-   Evaluate overall sales performance using key performance indicators
    (KPIs).
-   Compare sales across car models, vehicle segments, and regions.
-   Review monthly sales trends and identify periods of weaker
    performance.
-   Explore customer ratings, delivery times, and discounts.
-   Present findings in a clear dashboard to support data-driven
    decisions.

## Tools & Technologies

-   **Microsoft Excel** --- sales data preparation, analysis, and
    charts.
-   **Power BI** --- dashboard visualisation and KPI reporting.

## Dataset

The project covers sales data for **January--December 2025**. The report
describes a dataset with 1,000 orders, 1,110 cars sold, 12 models, 5
regions, 10 cities, and 10 salespeople.

## Dashboard KPIs

The project report highlights these measures: - **Total net sales:**
₹912.85 million - **Cars sold:** 1,110 - **Total orders:** 1,000 -
**Average customer rating:** approximately 4.0 out of 5 - **Average
delivery time:** approximately 5.4 days - **Average discount:**
approximately 4.5%

## Analysis & Key Insights

-   SUVs contribute the largest share of net sales, at about 39%.
-   XL6 and Jimny together account for approximately 27% of net sales.
-   South and North together contribute about 63% of net sales.
-   Average sales in November and December are about 27% lower than the
    January--October monthly average.
-   The report identifies opportunities to review discounting, improve
    sales in the East and Central regions, and improve delivery and
    customer experience.

## Recommendations

Based on the report, the main recommendations are to prioritise
high-performing models, investigate the November--December sales
decline, strengthen regional marketing and dealer reach, use targeted
offers instead of blanket discounts, and follow up on low customer
ratings and delivery delays.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how sales data can be organised and visualised
to identify performance patterns, business challenges, and opportunities
for improvement. The dashboard provides a concise view of sales, model
and regional performance, monthly trends, and customer experience.
